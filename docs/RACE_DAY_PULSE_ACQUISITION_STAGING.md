# Race Day Pulse — owner-approved acquisition direction and preview
## Product direction
Pulse is free public predictive analytics, not an odds tutorial or a tip sheet. Make Win Probability percentages prominent, use standard North American tote odds where displayed, and keep explanations brief and fact-based. No probability dot graphics. Hold weather/track-condition analysis and any surface-change editorial stories pending validated modeling; mixed surface/scratch examples are not eligible for the initial public feed. Hold outcomes, historical accuracy claims and public performance dashboard until TRI Gold is complete and evidence is approved. Continue source-verified scratch changes, new projected favorite, finishing order movement; pace advantage remains validation-gated. Preserve the existing mobile compact band, right desktop hero, approved logo, saddle chips, race ordinals, title capitalization and terminology. Do not change engine or production.

## Future research — market movement (owner direction, November 26)
Separate projected steam/drift analytics from TRI win probability. Steam means shortening actual tote odds; drift means lengthening actual tote odds. No projected direction or magnitude until authorized, timestamped historical tote snapshots exist, with an explicit forecast cutoff and target (e.g. 5 minutes before scheduled post vs last observable pre-off tote update). Normalize odds to implied market probability for analysis; preserve North American fractional tote notation in customer displays. Distinguish observed moves from forecasts and pool growth from scratch-driven reallocation; exclude unsettled scratch, conditional entry, stale quote, low-liquidity and source exceptions. Compare against no-change and market-only baselines with chronological out-of-sample tests; evaluate directional accuracy, magnitude error and calibration. No market-movement claims, scoring changes, new data purchases, or live feature until data permissions, evaluation and owner approval. Reserve complementary visual icon identities; show only verified two-point data, never an invented historical sparkline. This is roadmap documentation, not active research or a dependency for current Pulse launch. Gold and condition work remain independent.
