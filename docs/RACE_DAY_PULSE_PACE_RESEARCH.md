# Pace Advantage: research-only gate
November 26, 2026. The existing approved badge evaluator provides race-level Strong/Moderate/Weak pace and per-runner Pace Fit badges. These fields are not a causal attribution of score changes. This research compares only source-certified original and scratch-final pairs with matching active fields, identities, same surface/conditions/distance/rail and unchanged displayed trainer/jockey; known MTO/field/jockey holds remain excluded. Original badge provenance and final badge metadata are required. Report aggregate counts only.

Use the proportional survivor benchmark: original win probability divided by original surviving probability mass, times 100. A revised probability above that baseline is a residual, not evidence that pace caused the movement. A tentative research screen is improved Pace Fit and >=4 percentage points residual, optionally accompanied by a changed race-level pace state. The four-point screen is exploratory, not an approved public threshold. No public PACE_ADVANTAGE class or prose until certified evidence and owner-approved calibration. No claim about running style, lone speed, pressure, or causal contribution from badge changes alone. No historical results or retroactive inputs. No changes to TRI, F4, site, R2, or release flags.

Script: scripts/race_day_pulse_pace_research.py. Dedicated read-only workflow: .github/workflows/verify-pulse-pace-research.yml. The two user-supplied November 26 track ledgers are the initial sample, not the entire historical corpus. Synthetic tests include pure proportional redistribution, positive residual, missing pace badges, identity errors, condition and jockey changes. Future independent validation requires authentic paired cards and approved evidence for running styles before a customer-facing claim.
