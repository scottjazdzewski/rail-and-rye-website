## PROVISIONAL DISPLAY RULES UNDER TEST
- Favor an actual winner-ranking reversal among surviving runners, provided the original and revised 100%-mass field scores are source-certified.
- A scratched original top contender is usually an obvious leader change: de-prioritize it and require a meaningful new favorite PWIN increase (current provisional 4 percentage points).
- A surviving horse with the largest absolute TRI win-probability shift must move at least 4 percentage points to be featured.
- An F4 second-plus-third probability change must be at least 7 percentage points to qualify.
- Use at most six real alerts. Select the highest-impact alerts; take at most two per track on the first pass to encourage breadth; fill remaining slots from other genuine events, including additional alerts from one track.
- Do not produce an unverified early-pace causality story solely from a runner removal or a PWIN change.
- The winning probability/fair odds model itself remains frozen. No new outcomes, odds or predictions are generated by Pulse.
