INDEPENDENT RACING RESEARCH

The work behind the race.

01 / DATA

Begin with the evidence.

Every race is a new set of circumstances. We study past performances, form, surface, distance, race conditions, competition and connections to understand the full field—not just the most recognizable name.

02 / MODEL ENGINEERING

Give every runner a probability.

Our TRI framework brings engineered racing inputs together to estimate each starter’s chance of winning. The result is a field-wide probability assessment that can also be translated into fair odds.

03 / RACING INTELLIGENCE

Turn research into perspective.

A model's estimate is not a betting instruction. We consider where the numbers differ from market expectations and how changing race-day conditions may affect the picture.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Independent thinking. No guarantees.

There are no certainties in horse racing. The goal is clearer judgment, better questions and informed decisions—not a promise of winners.
