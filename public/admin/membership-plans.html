Stripe webhooks ↗

Webhook events to enable

On both existing Rail & Rye webhook endpoints (test and live), include customer.subscription.created, customer.subscription.updated, customer.subscription.deleted, customer.subscription.paused, customer.subscription.resumed, invoice.paid, invoice.payment_failed, and invoice.payment_action_required. Keep the existing checkout.session.completed event enabled.

Access begins after Stripe confirms the membership. Renewal and cancellation events update the member record; a scheduled reconciliation also checks active subscriptions. The site preserves access through the paid period after a cancellation and gives a three-day grace period for a failed renewal.

Customer billing settings

In Stripe, open Settings → Billing → Customer portal. Enable payment-method updates and set subscription cancellations to take effect at the end of the current billing period. Members can also cancel from their account page; their access stays active until the paid-through date.

The “Manage payment method” button needs the Stripe Customer Portal enabled. The site’s separate cancellation control already preserves access through the end of the paid term.
