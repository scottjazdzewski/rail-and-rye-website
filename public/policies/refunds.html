Refund Policy

Pre-launch draft — under review. This is preliminary policy text for Rail & Rye Racing's test website. Real-money payments are not enabled. The final effective terms may be updated following legal/business review.

Digital delivery

Rail & Rye Racing sells downloadable publications. A digital publication can be accessed immediately and may contain time-sensitive racing analysis. Please review the track, date, title, format and price before buying.

When we review refunds

Contact us about duplicate charges, receipt of the wrong file, corrupted or unusable content, an unresolved access or delivery failure, or another material mistake by Rail & Rye Racing. We will review requests and provide a refund or correction where appropriate or required by law.

Racing results

A racing selection, probability, forecast or opinion being incorrect does not by itself qualify a purchase for a refund. We do not guarantee racing outcomes or wagering profits.

How to get help

Write to support@railandryeracing.com with the purchase email, publication title, race date and a description of the issue. Never email card numbers or security codes.

Approved refunds

Approved refunds ordinarily return to the original payment method through the payment processor. The arrival time depends on your payment provider. Refunded publications may have access revoked. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be waived.
