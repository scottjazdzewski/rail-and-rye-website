Customer data and digital delivery

Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft — under review. This is preliminary policy text for Rail & Rye Racing's test website. Real-money payments are not enabled. The final effective terms may be updated following legal/business review.

Information collected

To process digital publication orders, we may collect purchaser email, product title, order and payment references, transaction amount, status, access and recovery history, dates and support communications. Security infrastructure may process technical request and browser information.

Service providers

Stripe processes card payments; Rail & Rye does not intend to store full card numbers or card verification codes. Cloudflare hosts our website and private PDF storage and provides security and inbound email forwarding. Resend is intended to send transactional delivery and recovery emails after V3 is enabled. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Purposes

We use information to confirm and fulfill purchases, provide access, recover eligible orders, process refunds, respond to support, investigate suspected abuse, reconcile payments and comply with applicable obligations. Purchases do not automatically enroll you in marketing emails.

Sharing and retention

We disclose information to necessary service providers or when required by law; we do not sell purchaser email lists to advertisers. We retain order and support records as reasonably necessary for service, fraud prevention, financial and legal purposes; download availability periods are distinct from record-retention periods.

Security, cookies and requests

Private PDFs are stored in nonpublic storage and served after authorization checks. Security and checkout services may require session cookies or technical signals. You can request information, correction or deletion where applicable by emailing support@railandryeracing.com; reasonable identity verification and lawful retention exceptions may apply.

Children and changes

The service is intended for adults. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data. We may revise this notice as our services and legal requirements evolve, with the updated effective date shown here.
