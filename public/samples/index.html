01RACE-DAY ANALYSIS

A field-by-field view of each race.

Race Cards organize the day race by race. A quick race profile sets the scene, then the runner table brings the entire field into one comparison.

  1. 1

    Race identity

    Track, date, race number, class, post time and field status tell you exactly which contest you’re looking at.

  2. 2

    How the race sets up

    Pace strength, surface and distance provide a compact read on the conditions each runner will face.

  3. 3

    Compare the whole field

    Each runner is shown with speed, form, class, pace and surface/distance indicators, plus estimated win probability and fair odds.

View available Race Cards ↗
02PRICE HORSES

Selected runners, with a reason at the price.

Longshot Radar highlights contenders whose odds may understate their case. Every entry is tied to a specific race, with a short explanation of how the horse could outperform expectations in today’s conditions.

  1. 1

    Price comparison

    Morning-line odds sit beside estimated win probability and fair odds, so you can compare the listed price with our assessment.

  2. 2

    Runner and race

    Horse name, track, race number and post time make each selection easy to find on the day’s card.

  3. 3

    The specific betting case

    A concise, horse-specific note connects form or race conditions to why the price is worth attention.

View Longshot Radar editions ↗
03FAVORITE & ALTERNATIVE

A closer look when the favorite can be challenged.

The Target List focuses on races where the morning-line favorite looks vulnerable and a specific alternative has a stronger, evidence-backed case. Each matchup shows both sides so the reasoning is clear.

  1. 1

    Race conditions

    Track, race, post time, surface and distance frame the matchup before you get to the analysis.

  2. 2

    Favorite versus alternative

    “ML Favorite” and “Attack” label the two horses, with their morning line, estimated win probability and fair odds shown side by side.

  3. 3

    Why the alternative merits attention

    The written case explains the favorite’s vulnerability and the challenger’s affirmative angle, including why the listed price may be compelling.
