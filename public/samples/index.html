01RACE-DAY ANALYSIS

Race Cards

A track-by-track view of the day, built around the full field.

What you’ll see inside

  • Track, date, race and runner information.
  • Estimated win probabilities and corresponding fair odds.
  • Form and race context, organized for race-day reference.

02PRICE HORSES

Longshot Radar

A shortlist of price horses assessed against our independent estimates.

What you’ll see inside

  • Selected runners with their track, race and post-time context.
  • Available market odds alongside estimated win probabilities and fair odds.
  • Concise reasoning that considers recent form and race conditions.

03HORSES TO FOLLOW

Stable Notes

Horses whose performances or circumstances may matter at their next start.

What you’ll see inside

  • Noteworthy past performances, layoff patterns or developing form.
  • Relevant changes in distance, surface, class and recent figures.
  • A clear note on why each horse is worth remembering.

04FAVORITE & ALTERNATIVE

Target List

A closer examination when a shorter-priced favorite faces a credible challenger.

What you’ll see inside

  • The favorite and a viable alternative supported by the form.
  • Odds, estimated win probabilities and fair odds for comparison.
  • The reasoning behind the matchup, including figures and distance or surface fit.
